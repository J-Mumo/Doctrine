## The believer's identity and the believer's reality in Christ
1. A believer in Christ: One who is born again, a recipient of God's life, a partaker of God's divine nature.
  John 3:16 For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

  #### Gifts that come with salvation
  1. The forgiveness of sins
    Ephesians 1:7 In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace.
  2. We are made the righteousness of God in Christ Jesus
    2 Corinthians 5:21 For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.
  3. We become active functional recipients of His life
    1 John 5:12-13 He that hath the Son hath life; and he that hath not the Son of God hath not life.
    These things have I written unto you that believe on the name of the Son of God; that ye may know that ye have eternal life, and that ye may believe on the name of the Son of God.

2. A believer in Christ is one who has been empowered to live like Christ and function like Christ. To live like Christ means you have His nature and you can manifest His character and to function like Christ means you have His wisdom and power and you can manifest God-like possibilities.
  John 1:12 But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name.

## The reality of today's world
1. Wickedness: 1 John 5:19 And we know that we are of God, and the whole world lieth in wickedness.

2. Tribulation: John 16:33 These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.

3. Temptations: Matthew 6:13 And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

4. Evil: : Matthew 6:13 And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.

5. The wickedness of man: Genesis 6:5 And GOD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.

The reality of greed, self, wickedness, pride, demonic influences over men, political instability, economic turmoil, all kinds of contentions against governments and institutions; all these together have left our world in need of the life, the power and the advantage of heaven.
