## Re-igniting revival fire
When revival fire fails and darkness seems to prevail, God first finds a man or finds a group of people who have not compromised. There will always be remnant.
He then places a spiritual dissatisfaction (burden) within their spirits.

Burden: A spiritual constraint, a strong feeling of heaviness, it talks about weight, unrest, dissatisfaction.

### Kinds of burdens
1. Demonic burdens, they are oppresive and destructive.
  Isaiah 10:27 And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.

2. Burdens of natural origin; human needs
  Galatians 6:2 Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

  Philippians 4:6 Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.

3. God sent, God initiated burdens.
  They are destiny activating burdens. They are connected to the birthing of your assignment and birthing og God's agenda or God's restorative plan.

The burdens comes with an intense grace for prayer and that leads to the rise of prophetic intercessors.
  2 Chronicles 7:14 If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.

  Ezekiel 22:30 And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.

The moment people begin to pray, they activate the ministry of angels.
What happens when you commit yourself to prayer:
- Encounters: People will start having radicle encounters.
- All kinds of soul winning activities.

Encounters are the ignition. What preserves the fire is the teaching ministry.

A strong vibrant and consistent prayer investment over any territory begins to stir up encounters. It brings brokeness and repentance and it ushers the evangelistic and the apostolic to begin to carry out their ministry in that territory.
The same prayer ministry releases the spirit of wisdom and revelation upon men and women to understand the scripture and the ways of God. This will bring rise to the teaching ministry.
A major part of the enlightenment, transformation, reformation, and the empowerement of the saints depends on the teaching ministry.

The rise of accurate, effective and balanced teaching ministries, will lead to the rise of strong, revived, vibrant, enlightened and transforemed believers, who can now shine as light and salt and become change agents in the society.

#### The order
Men pray -> God releases a burden ->  Men receive that burden and pray -> Encounters and soul winning activities begin to happen -> Apostolic ministry receives that and begins to mature the saints -> The cultivate in the saints the mentality of an apostolic christian.

Only revived men can revive territories, only transformed men can ransform territories.

For as long as prophetic intercessors keep the prayer fire burning, for as long as soul winning activities never cease, for as long as ministers of the gospel keep building, refining, empowering vibrant transformed and apostolic christians, and for as long as the king priest dominion strategy is practised, where believers are involved in the matters of the altar and matters of state, revival will never die.

### Elements of an effective teaching ministry
1. Doctrine: A body of knowledge that transforms a student to match the status of the teacher.
2. The principles of the kingdom.
3. Wisdom for living, to know how to walk as sheep among wolves.
4. Give the people a word compliant world-view.
