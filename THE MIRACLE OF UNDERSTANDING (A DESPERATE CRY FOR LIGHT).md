  1. The revelation of Jesus and His eternal plan.

  2. To draw out principles from scripture that will help you walk in victory.
    Romans 15:4 For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

  3. Acts by breathing upon your mind; ideas, inspired thoughts, which will be transformed to witty inventions and supernatural solutions.

  4. Activates the prophetic dimension in every believer, allowing you access to dreams, visions, supernatural encounters.
