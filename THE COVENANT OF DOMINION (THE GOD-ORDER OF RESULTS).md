Revelation 5:10 And hast made us unto our God kings and priests: and we shall reign on the earth.

1. Loving the Lord and learning how to walk in His ways are two connected but seperate experiences.
  Deuteronomy 11:22-25 For if ye shall diligently keep all these commandments which I command you, to do them, to love the Lord your God, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him;
  Then will the Lord drive out all these nations from before you, and ye shall possess greater nations and mightier than yourselves.
  Every place whereon the soles of your feet shall tread shall be yours: from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, even unto the uttermost sea shall your coast be.
  There shall no man be able to stand before you: for the Lord your God shall lay the fear of you and the dread of you upon all the land that ye shall tread upon, as he hath said unto you.

  Joshua 22:5 But take diligent heed to do the commandment and the law, which Moses the servant of the Lord charged you, to love the Lord your God, and to walk in all his ways, and to keep his commandments, and to cleave unto him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul.

2. Engaging with God goes beyond loving Him and having a sincere heart. It takes knowing how God operates with men on earth and learning how to align in His ways to successfully translate realities and possibilities to find expression on earth.

3. Every believer who truly desires exploits, dominion, kingdom relevance, must understand the concept of covenants.
  Exodus 19:5-6 Now therefore, if ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people: for all the earth is mine:
  And ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.

Covenant: There are two dimensions to understanding covenants
  - A set of laws, guides and rules that God used in dealing with people.
  - Heaven's modus operandi, one of the major ways of walking with God. This is first a mentality and also the highest dimension of partnership with God.

  A divine system of partnership between God and man, based on well defined terms and conditions and backed up by His integrity and power, upon which God is committed to keep amd make good His word when man fulfills His part.

  A covenant functions almost as a legal contract with terms and consequences; but unlike standard covenants there is a slight difference with our covenants with God.
  Differences:
    - God is still the one that supplies strength for man to keep His part of the covenant.
      Philippians 2:13 For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.

    - Every covenant with God still leaves room for the mercy of God to speak through the frailty of men.
      Lamentations 3:22-23 It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
      They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

    - God is the initiator and the defender of the terms that bind in the agreement while believers are the beneficiaries who must understand and fulfil their part of the term to see their performance.

  A covenant is a mentality of walking with and engaging with God where the believers understand that the desires from God have conditions that must be met in order to be made manifest.
  A covenant is a mentality of obedience and compliance to the scriptural conditions attached to manifesting kingdom results.

  Success, victory, dominion in the kingdom is not luck. It is not eve entirely based on the love of God. God loves everyone but our individual results will be a product of understanding the terms and conditions of dominion and obtaining grace to walk in keeping with those conditions.

  Dominion, success and impact is our inheritance in Christ, but it takes compliance to God's terms to make them a functional reality.

  In summary, a covenant is a revelation of God's compliance system for experiencing His power and His backing.

When you understand the mystrey of covenants, you have enetered into the God order of results.

God's love towards man is not based on covenants, it is based on His nature and His choice.
  Jeremiah 31:3 The Lord hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.

  2 Timothy 2:13 If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.

The extent of dominion, greatness, impact and trust with God that we command is based on covenant.

### Platforms for relational engagements
1. Emotions (weakest)
2. Principals: Principals do not demand knowing the person who created them, they demand understanding the working knowledge and engaging it.
3. Covenants: Demands a functional relationship with the covenantor, the working knowledge of the blessings that come with that covenant, the terms and conditions that releases those blessings to become your inheritance.

## The terms of the covenant (What does it take)
1. You must know and believe that a life of dominion and exploits is your inheritance in Christ accessed through salvation.
  Romans 5:2 By whom also we have access by faith into this grace wherein we stand, and rejoice in hope of the glory of God.

2. You need high level spiritual illumination, access to the knowledge of the ways of God, access to the principles of the kingdom.

3. You must understand the God-man partnership in making dominion a functional reality in your life. It means you have a price to pay.
  The God-man partnership is the realm of faith, believing God and obeying as proof that your truly believe.

4. You must understand the power and the value of process. 
  Process allows for growth and maturity.
    Mark 4:26-28 And he said, So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed into the ground;
    And should sleep, and rise night and day, and the seed should spring and grow up, he knoweth not how.
    For the earth bringeth forth fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, after that the full corn in the ear.

  Process builds patience and endurance
    Hebrews 6:15 And so, after he had patiently endured, he obtained the promise.

  Process helps you to understand the pathway to results so that you can mentor others.

  Process tames overconfidence and minimizes mistakes and regrets.

5. You must understand the purpose of dominion, influence, greatness and the purpose of blessings in the kingdom. 
  A major part of the covenant of dominion is understanding the purpose of everything God does with you and God gives to you.
  Purpose prunes and purifies desires. 
  Purpose kills the temptation for abuse; abuse of resources, opportunities, access, influence.
  Purpose brings longevity to impact.

  There is a 3 fold purpose to anything God does anf anything God gives us in the kingdom:
  1. To reveal His love, power and integrity among men thereby drawing many to Him.
  2. To bring consolation to the believer. To show through the believer the value of loving, seeking and serving God.
  3. To empower the believer to be a blessing to the world.
