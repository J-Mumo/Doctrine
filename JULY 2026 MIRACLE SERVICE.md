Believers in Christ are life giving spirits:
  John 10:10 The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.

  2 Corinthians 3:5-6 Not that we are sufficient of ourselves to think any thing as of ourselves; but our sufficiency is of God;
  6 Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.

  Romans 8:11 But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.

  1 Corinthians 15:45 And so it is written, The first man Adam was made a living soul; the last Adam was made a quickening spirit.

  1 John 4:17 Herein is our love made perfect, that we may have boldness in the day of judgment: because as he is, so are we in this world.

Life her is beyond existence. It is the summation of every component that upgrades a man to reflect the fullness of God in experience.

God desires that His people walk in the reality of dominion, purpose, fellowship with Him and with one another, and joy.
  Genesis 1:26 And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.

  2 Corinthians 13:14 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the communion of the Holy Ghost, be with you all. Amen.

  John 16:24 Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.

Achieving this will mean God will step in to situations providing supernatural solutions to issues that distract us.

  Matthew 7:7-8 Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:
  8 For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.

### Keys to enhance receiving from God
1. The consciousness of the goodness of God.
  Psalm 100:5 For the Lord is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.

  Psalm 136:1 O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

  Psalm 23:6 Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.

2. It is possible that inspite of the goodness of God, that the word of God can seem to fail in your life.
  Every time that the word of God seems to fail, these are the areas to check:
  1. Your desires vs the will of God. The power of God moves where the will of God is.
   Ways to extract the will of God:
   - The written word
   - The revelatory ministry of the Holy Spirit
   - The prophetic
  2. Verify divine timing. Timing is as powerful as purpose. 
    Psalm 102:13 Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion: for the time to favour her, yea, the set time, is come.

    Acts 2:1 And when the day of Pentecost was fully come, they were all with one accord in one place.

    Galatians 4:1-2 Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;
    2 But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.
  3. Your obedience of faith
    Hebrews 4:1-3 Let us therefore fear, lest, a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any of you should seem to come short of it.
    2 For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.
    3 For we which have believed do enter into rest, as he said, As I have sworn in my wrath, if they shall enter into my rest: although the works were finished from the foundation of the world.
  4. Demonic hinderances, there may be need for warfare.
    John 10:10 The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.

    Ephesians 6:16 Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.
