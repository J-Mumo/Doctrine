Your waiting is only gauranteed to produce profit if that waiting is upon the Lord.
### Unprofitable waitings
1. You can wait upon men, human connections; that is the waiting that leads to vanity and pain.
    Psalm 60:11 Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.
    Psalm 121:2 My help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth.

2. You can wait upon your intelligence and ideas.
    Proverbs 3:7 Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the Lord, and depart from evil.

    Ecclesiastes 9:11 I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.

## Why taryy, why wait?
1. Waiting upon the Lord provides the platform for the making, refining, remoulding of the man that desires to be used by the Lord. It allows God to minister upon the believer as the refiner, the maker and the purifier.
  We have all kinds of motifs, ambitions and desires locked up within our hearts and minds; so waiting upon the Lord allows God to prune and edit those motifs until all that is left in you is the desire to glorify Jesus. This is where flesh and self dies.
  Galatians 2:20 I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.

2. Waiting is the place of exchange. It is the place where divine transactions happen; where your weaknesses and limitations are exchanged for His strength, where your limited human perspectives and assessment of things are exchanged for divine perspective and a more accurate interpretation of things. It is at this place you gain stature and capacity to host tremendous dimensions of God's power and wisdom without it overwhelming you or destroying you.
  Isaiah 61:2-3 To proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord, and the day of vengeance of our God; to comfort all that mourn;
  To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness; that they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.

3. Waiting is a place of revelation and divine downloads; where mandates, patterns and strategies for your life and kingdom assignment are delivered. It is dangerous to experiment your life and destiny with every available idea or that which is a trend.
  Psalm 25:5 Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

  Jeremiah 33:3 Call unto me, and I will answer thee, and show thee great and mighty things, which thou knowest not.

## The dynamics of waiting
1. Assume a posture of surrender. Your focus must be more of yielding than presenting a list of desires and demands.

2. Waiting upon the Lord yields maximum results when it becomes a lifestyle than a circumstantial practice. Consistency powers the experience.
  Your desire to be with, and depend on God must not be circumstantial or a once in a while experience. It must be consistent and delightsome.
  Psalm 63:1 O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is;

  Psalm 42:1 As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God.

3. Your focus should be more on the quality and the intensity of the communion with God, not just the duration of the excercise.
