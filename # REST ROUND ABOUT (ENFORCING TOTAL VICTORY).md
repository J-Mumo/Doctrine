The bible does not hide the fact that believers can face challenges.

## Facts about mountains, afflications & unfavorable conditions
1. Love and seek the Lord beyond the desire to manifest testimonies and victories over those challenges.
  Job 1:20 Then Job arose, and rent his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshipped,

2. Realise challenges can and do arise on our way to manifest victory and dominion and that they happen for various reasons: ignorance, demonic attacks, process / natural course of things, prunings and refinings of God.

3. Know that challenges are not unusual; it is being defeated by the challenges that is unusual for the believer.

4. Your assignment as a believer in Christ, is to engage with the forces of victory and expect those challenges to turn into testimonies of triumph.

The bible brings us a word of hope that we can engage with God in a way that summounts all chalenges and establish victory in every area of our lives.

## Keys to rest round about
1. Believe in the possibility of all round rest.

2 Believe in and submit to Jesus, the author and the basis of true rest.
  Matthew 11:28 Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

3. You must believe in the responsibility of enforcing rest on every side.
  Hebrews 4:9-11 There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.
  For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.
  Let us labour therefore to enter into that rest, lest any man fall after the same example of unbelief.

  Philippians 2:12 Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

4. You must study, you must understand and you must engage the unique kingdom principles connected to every are you seek to experience victory and rest.

5. You must understand and engage in strategic spiritual warfare.
  1 Corinthians 16:9 For a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries.

  Adversaries come in 3 fold:
  - Spirit entities: Deal with spirits by binding them. Resist the devil and he will flee from you.
  - Systems: You learn influence and be a power broker. You pray that God will lift you through capacity, value to become part of those who have access to the corridors of power.
  - Men: You deploy the wisdom of the serpent. You learn the law of honor, relationships, the principles of adaptation.

  The strategy for warfare is watch and pray. There is a strategy for warfare that is mind dependent and another on pray. Watching helps you to know how to pray well.

6. Patience
  1 Peter 5:10 But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.

  Hebrews 6:15 And so, after he had patiently endured, he obtained the promise.

  Galatians 6:9 And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

7. You must practise praise and thanksgiving as a lifestyle.
  Psalm 67:6-7  Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us.
  God shall bless us; and all the ends of the earth shall fear him
